Pick a seed, plant an empty plot, then harvest ripe crops. Neighbouring crops change yield.

Field guide

Every planted crop checks all eight surrounding plots, diagonals included. Each same-crop neighbour halves yield (multiplicative). Each nearby crop of a different type that is not disliked gives +6%; a liked neighbour adds +20% and a disliked neighbour subtracts 20% (once each). Three or more different neighbours add +8%. Yield banks while the crop grows — the bottom-left counter is the amount waiting to be harvested.

HARMONY above the field is the value-weighted average of these multipliers.
